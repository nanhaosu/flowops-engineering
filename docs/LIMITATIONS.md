# Boundaries

- One declared synthetic park and related parameter profiles; no calibration to a real plant.
- Volume-only physical model. Water classes are coarse admission categories, not chemical compatibility or treatment-compliance evidence.
- Synthetic owner roles and acknowledgements; no industrial authentication, control integration or authority to stop production.
- Batch timing, residual curves, manufacturing rates and cleaning-water quantities are assumed. No site-specific operating expenditure or financial return is calculated.
- Public-only forecasts hold declared conditions and cannot foresee unpublished disturbances.
- The common advice policy has retained unnecessary-interruption counterexamples. It is not an optimal scheduler or a field operating recommendation.
- Zero independent participants. A state-table comparison cannot be promoted to measured time savings, lower omission rates or willingness to adopt.
- The public-document transfer case cannot be run honestly with the available operational data and treatment topology. Its unknowns remain unresolved.

The project is preserved as an inspectable engineering artifact. Further feature development was paused because actual incremental user value remains unestablished.
