# A documented coordination requirement and an unsuccessful transfer boundary

The government-hosted public EIA for a food-processing project in Tangqian Green Industrial Park, Yongtai, provides a concrete requirement: when an enterprise cannot immediately stop its process after a park wastewater incident, it should communicate subsequent wastewater quantities and remain coordinated with the park facility. Stored wastewater is returned to an equalization tank for re-treatment, and repair completion is a prerequisite to production resumption.

Source: [Yongtai government-hosted EIA, PDF page 77 / printed page 74](https://www.yongtai.gov.cn/xjwz/zwgk/zfxxgkzdgz/hjbh/hjyxpj/202210/P020221008386868637895.pdf#page=77). Historical project status is on PDF page 59 / printed page 56: at the report's survey time, the shared plant had not started operating. The document is not evidence of a dated incident or its current 2026 status.

The document describes 125 m³ and 210 m³ tanks, but current occupancy, connectivity, available capacity, flows and residual water are unknown. Its separate sections report 141.635 and 113.635 t/day of production wastewater; the scope of this difference was not resolved. Neither figure was silently adopted as an instantaneous flow.

This was a document-grounded task reconstruction with explicitly authored hypothetical messages. It was not a real incident replay. A competent editable table can record the task. The current prototype has relevant notification and recovery mechanisms, but lacks the actual inputs and a complete equalization/re-treatment route. No real-site import, live performance, water-quality benefit or human comparison was claimed.
